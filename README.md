# FLEUR – User Guide

A flower shop management website with AI assistance. Open `index.html` in a browser to use it. No installation needed.

## Quick start
1. **Discover Flowers**: the AI suggests flowers that fit the current season. Click **Add to Inventory** to add one to your stock (you can change the quantity), **Save for Later** to keep it for later, or **Dismiss** to hide it. Each time you add flowers, some new orders may be generated.
2. **Orders**: click **Confirm** to accept an order, then **Open in shop** to make the bouquet. For Freestyle orders, click **Suggest designs** and choose one.
3. **Virtual Flower Shop**: click or drag flowers onto the wrapping station to match the order's recipe. Choose a wrap, basket or box, then the paper and ribbon colors, and click **Finish bouquet**.
4. Go back to **Orders**: click **Hand to courier**, then **Mark delivered**. Revenue will then appear in **Analytics**.

## Other pages
- **Overview**: a summary of revenue, profit, alerts and AI suggestions.
- **Seasonal Insights**: current season, recommended flowers, high-demand flowers and seasonal opportunities.
- **Inventory**: view stock, receive new batches and record wastage.
- **AI Insights**: approve restocking plans, check freshness and apply AI-suggested prices.
- **Settings**: switch language (English / Tiếng Việt), adjust your budget, and clear all data with **Reset All Data**.

Your data is saved in your own browser and is not sent anywhere.
